# Goals

Current outbound/pipeline goals for [[me/role|James's]] work at [[business/firmatrust|FirmaTRUST]]:

1. Book 10 discovery calls per month from cold outreach and LinkedIn combined.
2. Get a 20%+ reply rate on cold emails by narrowing to one clear pain point per send.
3. Grow LinkedIn engagement enough to generate 5 inbound conversations a month without direct outreach.
4. Convert 30% of booked calls into a second meeting or proposal.
5. Build a repeatable weekly cadence, a fixed number of emails sent, posts published, follow-ups sent, so pipeline doesn't depend on one good week.

These map directly to [[me/preferences|priorities]]: pipeline that closes and a repeatable cadence over one-off wins.
