# Agentic AI Certification: Claude Code, Hermes Agent & Evals for PMs

Maven course by Dmitry Shapiro (CEO MindStudio, ex-CTO MySpace) and Dr. Marily Nika (GenAI Product Builder @ Google, ex-Meta). Direct competitor to [[me/goals|James Williams's Maven cohort]], narrower PM-specific angle.

## Format
$1,999. 3 weeks, 8 live sessions, 2-4 hrs/week live + 1-6 hrs/week projects. Lifetime access to recordings.

## Audience
Product leaders and senior PMs, founders/operators wanting to automate workflows with agents. No coding experience required.

## Signals
4.4/5 rating over 96 reviews (highest review volume of the three). Cohort Sep 21-Oct 9, 2026.

## Positioning
Claude Code fundamentals in week 1, then pivots to a third-party tool (Hermes Agent / MindStudio) for deployment and eval frameworks in weeks 2-3 — not a pure-Claude curriculum. Highest price of the three direct competitors.
