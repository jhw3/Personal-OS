# Brand Identity

## Company Name
mAxIm — AI Automated Solutions

## What We Do
Builds AI agents and automated workflows that take over tedious, repetitive work (data entry, invoice processing, content, reporting) so client teams get 10+ hours a week back and can focus on judgment calls and relationships.

## Who We Serve
Small and mid-size businesses whose teams lose 10-15 hours a week to manual, repetitive processes.

## Tone of Voice
Casual, professional, witty. Direct claims backed by real numbers, short paragraphs, no corporate speak, ends with a real question instead of a hard sell. Full voice spec: soul.md.

## Visual Identity
Steel Blue #4682B4 (primary), Arc Blue #4DA0FF (accent), Bright Core #37FAFC (glow highlight), Onyx #0A0C10 (text), Gunmetal Gray/Abyss (dark panels). Font: Montserrat (Bold for headings). Logo: brand/images/maxim_logo_transparent_subtitle.png. Full spec: brand/config/brand-config.md.

## Things We Never Say
Corporate filler ("circle back," "synergy," "let's dive in"), generic AI disclaimers, hype without a concrete result behind it.
