# Unitree

Chinese humanoid robotics maker, first in the sector to IPO (Shanghai, Aug 2026, ~$9B valuation). Only major humanoid player that's profitable — 5,500+ units shipped in 2025, G1 at $13,500-$16,000, R1 at $5,900. Wins on price-to-performance and proven unit economics, not AI capability.
