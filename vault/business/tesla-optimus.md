# Tesla Optimus

Tesla's humanoid robot program. Betting on manufacturing scale reuse (converted Model S/X lines at Fremont, dedicated Optimus factory at Giga Texas) and Tesla's FSD-derived AI stack to hit mass-market pricing ($20k-$30k target).

1,000+ Gen 3 units on the Fremont floor as of Jan 2026, but Musk himself called them "primarily for learning, not productive tasks." Optimus 3 production targeted to start Summer 2026.
