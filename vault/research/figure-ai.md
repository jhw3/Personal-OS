# Figure AI

Best-funded pure-play humanoid robotics company. Not a FirmaTRUST BD prospect — general market research requested by James on the humanoid robotics race.

## Background
Founded by Brett Adcock. Ended its OpenAI partnership to build Helix, an in-house vision-language-action model. Building both the robot (Figure 03) and the brain (Helix) in-house, plus its own manufacturing line (BotQ).

## Signals
- $1.9B+ raised total, $39B post-money valuation after Series C — most capitalized pure-play humanoid company on the planet.
- 11-month pilot at BMW Spartanburg: 90,000+ sheet-metal parts loaded, supporting 30,000+ X3 vehicles built. BMW and Figure now evaluating Figure 03 for expanded use.
- Demonstrated a 30-hour fully autonomous warehouse shift — 38,000+ packages, self-recovery via onboard cameras and Helix-02, zero human intervention.
- BotQ manufacturing ramp: a few units/month in 2025 → ~60 in Feb 2026 → ~240 in April 2026 (one robot every 90 minutes). Targeting 12,000 units/year run rate.
- Figure 03 (Oct 2025) redesigned ground-up for mass manufacturing and home environments, not just industrial.

## Edge
Full-stack vertical integration (robot + AI model + factory) with the deepest capital base in the sector. Real enterprise deployment data (BMW) beats most competitors still in pilot/demo stage.

## Likelihood of winning
High on the "best-funded AI-first generalist" track. Biggest risk is execution: pre-revenue at a $39B valuation, and BotQ's ramp (240/month) is still far from the 12,000/year target. If Helix keeps improving and BotQ scales on schedule, Figure is positioned to lead the enterprise/industrial segment. Watch the Figure 03 home-environment push — that's the bet that could expand the total addressable market beyond factories.

## Suggested angle
Case study material for FirmaTRUST content: "vertical integration as a moat" applies directly to how FirmaTRUST pitches full-stack AI agent builds vs. stitched-together point solutions.
