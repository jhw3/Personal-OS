First run of the rebuilt `/content-machine` (3-agent pipeline). Source: [[research/figure-ai]], [[research/tesla-optimus]], [[research/unitree]].

## Research brief (Researcher pass)
- Figure AI: $39B valuation, $1.9B+ raised, pre-revenue, real BMW deployment (90k+ parts loaded).
- Tesla Optimus: 1,000+ units on the Fremont floor, Musk called them "primarily for learning, not productive tasks."
- Unitree: $9B IPO (first humanoid maker to go public), 5,500+ units shipped 2025, the only one of the three actually profitable.
- **The insight:** funding size and valuation don't predict who's actually generating revenue. The least-hyped company is the one making money. Direct parallel to how SMBs should evaluate AI vendors: don't buy the valuation, buy the working system.
