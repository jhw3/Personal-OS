# Market Pulse — 2026-07-29

First pulse. [[projects/market-pulse/status|Market Pulse]] just went live. [[business/competitors/watchlist|Watchlist]] is empty, so this is a generic market scan, not targeted competitor intel — add real names to get sharper reads.

## Top signal
The SMB AI consulting segment is growing 25.7%/yr, outpacing enterprise — but 51% of small business AI adopters are stuck in an "experimenting, no real implementation" phase. That gap is the opening: this is the client profile FirmaTRUST should be pitching, and "customization / actual implementation" beats "generic tool rollout" as the pitch.

## Other signals
- Global AI consulting market: $14.1B in 2026, projected $116.8B by 2035 (26.5% CAGR) — market's not slowing down.
- 82% of small business employers have already invested in *some* AI tool, per the Small Business & Entrepreneurship Council — the buying motion is normalized, the gap is in follow-through.
- Named agencies coming up in searches for this space: Markovate, HatchWorks AI, Oxagile, DataRoot Labs, InData Labs, Cohevo, ELIYA, theautomators.ai. None of these are on the watchlist yet — worth a look to see who's actually competing for the same SMB deals as FirmaTRUST.
- Trend: SMBs increasingly unwilling to pay a premium for simple software setup since AI is automating that layer — spend is shifting to business process re-engineering. Reinforces "judgment calls and workflow redesign" as the value prop, not tool installation.
